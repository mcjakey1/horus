## Checked and consistent (no finding)

- **CSS tokens.** Every custom property the plans name exists in `src/styles.css`: `--ink-2`, `--ink-3`, `--line-2`, `--card`, and the operation `--diff`, `--group`, `--seq` and `--infer` families.
- **Existing frontend exports.** `Box`, `tileBox` and `tileHTML` (`src/reference/tile.ts`), `mountSearch` (`src/search.ts`) and `mountReference` all exist as the plans consume them.
- **`Hit` JSON.** It is `{"kind":"technique",…}` or `{"kind":"reference",…}` in `src-tauri/src/search.rs` and `src/api.ts`, as P2 T6 and P5 T7 assume.
- **`Edit` variants.** P2's base set, P3 T9's taxonomy variants (`Update*`, `LinkTechnique`, `UnlinkTechnique`, `AddBranch`, `RemoveBranch`) and P4 T1's link and board variants don't collide. `Touched` and `Change` grow additively: `links` and `boards` in P4, `integrity` in P6.
- **Undo routing.** Every change to library or taxonomy data in P2, P3, P4 and P6 goes through `edit::apply` and `History`. The things that aren't undoable are declared as such: export (P4), settings and the Find similar toggles (P5), and view scope (P4). P3 T11's `dev_seed_tags` is debug-only.
- **Keyboard maps.** They are scoped by focus, so they don't conflict:
  - **Reference view:** `1`–`9` pin callouts (P1); `L` opens the lightbox (P2); `b`, `k` and Shift+Enter (P4).
  - **Picker:** `1`–`4` choose the operation (P2).
  - **Radial:** `1`–`4` switch the operation (P3).
  - **Lightbox:** `0`, `1`, `+`, `-` and `o` (P4).
  - **Canvas views:** `0`, `+` and `-` (P3).
- **The shell.** P3 T2's `shell.register` handles both orders for P2's Inbox, and P4 T5 registers `"boards"`, which is already a `ViewId`.
- **SPEC coverage.** Every SPEC requirement maps to a plan task except #10 (board scope in the graph views), #16 (DOM speed at 2,000+), #17 (WCAG) and #25 (Compare from the graph views).
